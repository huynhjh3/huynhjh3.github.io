Business Question

What Drives High Airbnb Ratings?

Airbnb ratings are a key signal of trust, quality, and guest satisfaction. For hosts, stronger ratings can improve listing visibility, booking confidence, and long-term competitiveness.

This project analyzed Los Angeles Airbnb listings to identify which amenities, host characteristics, property types, and listing attributes were most associated with higher guest ratings.

Data Overview

Preparing Airbnb Listing Data

40,227

Unique Los Angeles Airbnb listings after cleaning and combining datasets.

Data Cleaning

Removed duplicates, dropped invalid review records, cleaned price fields, converted date fields, and standardized amenities.

Feature Engineering

Created features such as amenities count, host experience years, listing age, Superhost status, availability, and property type indicators.

Exploratory Analysis

Understanding Rating Patterns

Ratings Were Highly Skewed

Most listings were clustered between 4.5 and 5.0 stars, making continuous rating prediction difficult.

Price Was Not Enough

Higher prices did not consistently translate into better ratings, suggesting service quality mattered more than price alone.

Amenities Mattered

Listings with more convenience-focused amenities tended to receive stronger guest ratings.

Modeling Approach

Regression vs Classification

Regression Models

Tested Linear Regression, Ridge, Lasso, Random Forest Regressor, and Gradient Boosting Regressor to predict numerical review scores.

Classification Models

Converted ratings into a binary high-rating outcome where listings with review scores greater than or equal to 4.5 were labeled as high-rated.

Why Classification Worked Better

Because ratings were compressed near the top of the scale, classification provided a more practical way to distinguish stronger listings from lower-rated ones.

Key Findings

What the Analysis Revealed

Amenities Were a Major Signal

More amenities were associated with stronger ratings, suggesting guests value convenience and completeness.

Host Quality Mattered

Superhost status, host experience, and host responsiveness were important predictors of stronger listing performance.

Property Type Influenced Satisfaction

Entire homes and similar private property types performed better than more limited or shared accommodations.

Price Was Not a Guarantee

Pricing alone did not strongly explain high ratings, reinforcing that perceived service quality mattered more than simply charging more.

Business Recommendations

How Hosts Can Improve Listing Performance

Increase Useful Amenities

Add essential and convenience-focused amenities that improve the guest experience.

Improve Host Professionalism

Maintain strong communication, faster response times, and consistent service quality.

Highlight Listing Strengths

Emphasize amenities, property type, host experience, and unique features in the listing presentation.

Align Price With Service

Pricing should reflect service quality and guest expectations rather than relying on price as a standalone quality signal.

Limitations

What the Model Could Not Fully Capture

Airbnb ratings were highly compressed between 4.5 and 5.0, which limited the performance of regression models. Public listing data also does not include guest demographics, review text sentiment, or detailed behavioral history.

As a result, this project is best interpreted as a predictive analysis of observable listing attributes, not a causal explanation of what directly creates higher ratings.
