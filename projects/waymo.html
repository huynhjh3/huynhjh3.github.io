Business Question

Can Autonomous Vehicles Improve Urban Road Safety?

As autonomous vehicle deployments expand across major U.S. cities, policymakers and transportation agencies face an important question: do autonomous vehicles meaningfully improve roadway safety outcomes?

This project evaluated whether Waymo expansion into San Francisco and Los Angeles was associated with measurable changes in crash rates compared to similar California cities.
