Executive Summary

Business Problem

Leadership needed a clearer way to evaluate workload distribution, capacity pressure, turnaround time, staffing gaps, and future workforce needs during an operating model transition. The goal was to turn operational intake data into a repeatable workforce planning framework.

Analytical Workflow

Methodology

1. Data Validation

Filtered completed intake records, corrected missing or out-of-order timestamps, and standardized lifecycle stages.

2. TAT Engineering

Created assignment, pre-work, execution, closure, and total turnaround time metrics to understand workflow delays.

3. Capacity Modeling

Compared forecasted demand against estimated monthly capacity to identify utilization pressure and staffing gaps.

4. Core-Flex Strategy

Mapped teams into stable core, flexible delivery, and floating pool recommendations based on demand variability.
